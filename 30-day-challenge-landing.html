How it works

Three steps. Every day.

📖

Pick the day's topic

One prompt a day, with guiding questions ready when your mind goes blank.

🗣️

Speak out loud for 5–10 minutes

At your desk, in the shower, or while walking. Mistakes are fine — just keep talking.

✅

Check off your day

Mark today done and watch your streak grow, one day at a time.

Some days will feel hard. Don't stop when you make mistakes — just extend your thoughts a little more each time.
