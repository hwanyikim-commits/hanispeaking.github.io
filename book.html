Does this sound like you?

You know the grammar, but using it is another step.

The problem isn't that you need more grammar rules. You need a clearer path from recognizing a pattern to using it in your own Korean.

You understand Korean grammar when you see it in a sentence. But when you want to speak or write, creating sentences of your own still takes too much thought.

You may recognize expressions like -(으)ㄴ/는 것 같다 or -게 되다. But using them naturally in your own sentences is another step.

So you hesitate. You say less. You practice less. And little by little, the grammar you've learned stays in your head instead of turning into Korean you can actually use.

Real Korean Patterns helps you make that transition — one pattern at a time.

About Hani

I made this for the moment after the grammar explanation.

I'm Hani, a Korean teacher and the creator of Hani Speaking. In my work with Korean learners, I've seen the same gap again and again: learners understand what an expression means, but hesitate when they have to choose the right pattern, express the right nuance, and build a sentence of their own.

Real Korean Patterns is designed to close that gap. Each chapter takes one everyday pattern through context, natural examples, comparison, cultural notes, and guided practice.

The goal is simple: to help you turn the Korean you know into Korean you can use.
