WHY HANI SPEAKING

Why I created Hani Speaking

I've taught Korean for over seven years, and I kept seeing the same problem. Learners studied a lot, but still struggled to speak naturally.

I realized they didn't need more materials.

They needed more chances to actually use Korean.

So I created Hani Speaking, not to teach you more Korean, but to help you practice the Korean you already know until it feels like your own.

Learn the patterns. Understand the context. Practice every day.
