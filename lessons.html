Who is this class for?

Made for learners who want to move beyond studying Korean and start using it in their own lives.

📘 You understand, but can't speak

You know vocabulary and grammar, yet the words don't come naturally in conversation.

🗣 You need a speaking environment

You want regular conversation practice but don't have a Korean-speaking partner or consistent guidance.

🧭 You feel lost about studying

You're unsure what to practice first, how to organize your learning, or how to make progress.

☕ You want real-life Korean

You'd rather discuss your day, feelings, and experiences than repeat textbook conversations.

🌱 You prefer a relaxed space

You learn best with patient support, practical feedback, and room to make mistakes.

My teaching approach

Learn by actually using Korean

Instead of following the same textbook for everyone, I build each class around you and the Korean you want to use.

🗣

Conversation-based learning

Speak and write about real situations using personalized materials prepared for each lesson, with detailed 1:1 feedback.

🔁

Consistency over cramming

🧠

Core grammar and patterns

✍️

Learn through writing and speaking

Turn what you've learned into language you can use, until forming Korean sentences feels more familiar.

Personalized to your level

What will we do in class?

Each level is a starting point. Your lessons are adjusted to your interests, needs, and goals.

🌟 Build a foundation

Beginner

Move from knowing basic grammar to making your own sentences.

  • Learn essential grammar and everyday expression patterns for conversation.
  • Talk about your routine, what you did today, and what you're thinking.
  • Build confidence speaking in familiar situations.
Daily practiceShort writing and speaking tasks, plus simple Korean vocabulary quizzes (up to 30 minutes).
🐤 Speak more naturally

Intermediate

Express your experiences and opinions beyond short textbook answers.

  • Explore your interests and Korean or global topics through content you enjoy.
  • Combine reading, listening, writing, and speaking.
  • Practice more natural ways to share your thoughts and experiences.
Daily practiceUse practical expressions in your own sentences with short reading, listening, writing, and speaking tasks.
✨ Go deeper

Advanced

Develop clearer, more confident Korean for longer conversations.

  • Read Korean books and articles together.
  • Discuss complex topics, ideas, and opinions in Korean.
  • Practice long-form speaking and discussion with a focus on clarity and natural expression.
Personalized focusLesson content and speaking practice are adapted to your goals and the topics you want to explore.
