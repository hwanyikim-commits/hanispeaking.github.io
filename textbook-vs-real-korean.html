What's inside

10 sentences you probably learned

Here are the textbook versions. The PDF shows what Koreans say instead, and why.

  1. 01Calling someone “당신”TEXTBOOK당신은 어디에 살아요?
  2. 02Saying “let's” with -(으)ㅂ시다TEXTBOOK같이 밥을 먹읍시다.
  3. 03Asking what someone wantsTEXTBOOK무엇을 먹고 싶습니까?
  4. 04Talking about your own planTEXTBOOK저는 내일 친구를 만날 것입니다.
  5. 05Promising something to the person in front of youTEXTBOOK제가 하겠습니다.
  6. 06Guessing what is happeningTEXTBOOK아마 비가 올 것입니다.
  7. 07Reacting to what someone saysTEXTBOOK정말입니까?
  8. 08Giving a reasonTEXTBOOK왜냐하면 배가 아프기 때문입니다.
  9. 09Agreeing with someone's feelingTEXTBOOK저도 그렇게 생각합니다.
  10. 10When something bad already happenedTEXTBOOK괜찮습니다.
